## A note you can point at
@ plan
| md:Order
A codecast often needs a page beside the view: a few steps, a table, a small drawing. The voice could show that page, but it could not point into it. Now it can.
! the-trip
Every piece of the page has a name. A heading is named by its own words.
! the-trip/2
A step is named by its heading and its number. Here is step two.
sp: And the table under it?
! len
A row is named by its first cell. This is the row of the length.
! size
A column is named by its header.
! m2
The page has a second tab, with an exchange drawn in it. I name its second message: the tab opens by itself, and the arrow glows.
! on-the-wire
Naming a tab opens it too, so here is the frame again.
! written-once
Last, a framed remark is named by its title. This one says where the note lives. We will come back to that.
? How do I find the name of a thing?
  Take its words in lower case, and put a dash where there is a space. If a name is wrong, codecast check answers with every name the page has, so the right one is one copy away.
? Does the viewer still control the tabs?
  Yes. A click picks a tab as on any page. The codecast only takes over when a new sentence points somewhere else.
