## The player
@ code
+ player
At the bottom of the screen, this bar is the player. The space bar pauses and resumes, and the arrow keys go back or forward one sentence.
+ caption
In the middle of the bar, what is said is written too, so you can read along, or read instead of listening.
+ parts
Just above the text, the parts of the codecast. The full tour plays them all in a row; you can also play one alone.
+ voice
On the bottom left, the voice: a recording made with a natural voice, like this one, the voice of your browser, or no voice at all, to read at your own pace.
+ lang
Right next to it, the language. A codecast can be translated, and spoken in another language.
