## Around the app
@ code
+ search
At the top right, the search box. Type part of a name, and the item list keeps only what matches.
+ codecast
To its right, this button starts or stops a codecast. A project without one gets a built-in tour, written from its own code.
+ tour
Next to it, this one plays the full tour, from the first part to the last.
+ generate
Then, when the app runs on your machine, the Generate page records a codecast with a natural voice, in several languages.
+ settings
In the top right corner, the settings change the theme, the fonts, what the views show, the voice, and the language.
+ help
And just left of it, the question mark brings this tutorial back whenever you want.
